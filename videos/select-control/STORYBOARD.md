## Video direction

A Control key and a blue text selection are the repeated visual idea. Native page and result card layers show the workflow. Motion follows selection, press, reveal, language change and copy. Light scenes match the app; darker purple scenes reuse the approved icon palette. Clear cuts change the shot while narration continues. Music is original local synthesis.
