# Narration

Local Kokoro Adam (am_adam), speed 1.15. Speech determines scene timing unless the scene has a fixed window.

## s01: Good translation. Too many steps.

AI can make translation feel more natural. So why does getting there still take so many steps?

## s02: The copy-paste loop

Select. Copy. Switch tabs. Paste. Ask for a translation. Then find your place again. One small question becomes a break in your flow.

## s03: Meet AI Translator

Meet AI Translator for Chrome. Gemini translation, right where you read. A free extension built around one simple action.

## s04: Select. Tap Control.

Select the words you want. Tap and release Control. Your translation appears beside the text. Keep the original in view, and keep going.

## s05: Meaning first

Because useful translation is about meaning. Not just swapping words. An everyday phrase can become something that sounds natural in your language.

## s06: Read with confidence

A work message. A useful article. Details for your next trip. Translate the part you need, without moving your whole task somewhere else.

## s07: A little. Or a lot.

A word, a sentence, or a whole paragraph. You choose the selection. Get the answer in place, while the rest of the page stays familiar.

## s08: Your language. Your choice.

Choose from one hundred and ten language options. Need another language for this result? Change it in the card. Your saved default stays the same.

## s09: Keep the useful parts together

A new phrase. A new question. Translate as you go, with each result close to its source. No growing stack of translation tabs to manage.

## s10: Use the result

Copy a translation when you need it. Open the toolbar popup to read the latest result again during your current browser session.

## s11: Fits the way you work

Prefer a different key? Choose it in Settings. Or use the right-click menu. Pick your language and Gemini model. Make the workflow yours.

## s12: Free extension. Less friction.

The extension is free and open source, with no ads or analytics. Only text you select is sent to Google for the translation you request.

## s13: Keep your flow

Less copying. Less switching. More understanding. From the first unfamiliar phrase to the next useful idea, translation stays part of what you are doing.

## s14: Install once

To get started, find AI Translator in the Chrome Web Store. Choose Add to Chrome, then Add extension to confirm. That's it. Now you're ready for setup.

## s15: Get your Gemini key

Open Settings. Get your Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. Review the data details, then choose Agree and connect to Google.

## s16: Paste. Choose. Finish.

Paste your key. Choose your target language and model. Finish setup, then refresh your open pages. Your key stays saved on this device.

## s17: Select. Control. Understand.

You're ready. Select text. Tap Control. Understand, and keep going. AI Translator for Chrome.
