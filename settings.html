Set up AI Translator

Follow these steps here in Settings. You need your own Google API key to translate.

  1. Get your own API key. Open Google AI Studio, sign in, and create a key. Review data sharing below, then paste your key. Read the step-by-step guide for help.
  2. Choose your target language and model. Pick the language you want translations in. We recommend Gemini Flash-Lite when it is available in your model list.
  3. Save and start translating. Use Finish setup at the bottom of this page. Then select text on a web page and press Control, or choose Translate Selected Text from the right-click menu.

Before you connect to Google

Google receives your API key to check available models. When you request a translation, Google also receives your selected text and target language, including text from local files. AI translations can be wrong.

Your key is saved encrypted in this Chrome profile and opens automatically after restarts. No extra password is needed. Someone with full access to your browser profile may still recover it. Read the privacy details.

Required in every country: use a Gemini API key from a project with active billing. Keep optional sharing of this extension's request logs and datasets with Google off. With these settings, Google's paid terms exclude product improvement use of prompts and responses; safety and legal processing still applies. The extension is free, but Google API charges may apply.

Use conditions. By using Gemini through this extension, you agree to follow the requirements above and Google's terms. Use is for people aged 18 or older for professional or business purposes, in supported regions. The extension does not verify your billing or sharing settings.

Help and recovery

If a model is unavailable, refresh models, choose one from the list, and save preferences. If the list cannot load, check your key and connection, then try Refresh models.

Chrome settings pages and the Chrome Web Store do not allow translation. For local files, enable Allow access to file URLs in Manage Extension. After an extension update or reload, refresh open web pages. Reloading can interrupt a translation.

Privacy

Translation is not offline. After you agree above, your key is sent directly to Google for model checks. Selected text, including text from local files, is sent with your target language only when you request a translation. Your saved key is encrypted in this Chrome profile. Your language, model, and trigger preference can sync through Chrome. The extension keeps only the latest result for this browser session and does not keep translation history.

You can review data sharing above and withdraw your agreement at any time. This stops model checks and translations until you agree again. Your saved key is kept unless you choose Remove saved key.
