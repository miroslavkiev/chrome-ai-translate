About AI Translator

Super easy translation: select text and press Control.

Made for people

I created AI Translator to help people read, learn, and connect across languages. I hope it is useful to people around the world.

The extension translates the text you select using your own Google Gemini API key. You choose what to send and which language to read.

Tap and release Control to translate your selection. Control is the default key and can be changed in Settings. You can also use the page's right-click menu.

Manually tested on Mac only. Windows and Linux have not been manually tested.

Privacy and costs

Your API key is encrypted on this device and is not synced. It stays available across normal Chrome restarts without an unlock password. Someone with access to the browser profile may still recover it.

Before the first Google request, Settings explains data sharing and asks you to choose Agree and connect to Google. After that, selected text is sent directly to Google only when you ask for a translation. Model checks send your key but no selected text. The extension has no analytics, advertising, or translation history.

Review the details in Settings at any time. Choose Withdraw data agreement to stop new requests while keeping your encrypted key and preferences, or Remove saved key to delete the key and model cache. Data already sent cannot be recalled.

Translations are generated by AI and can be wrong. Check important wording before relying on it.
